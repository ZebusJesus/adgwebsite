Atlas Design Group

Engineering that makes the rest of the team stronger.

Founded in 2019, Atlas Design Group is a minority-owned structural and civil engineering practice. We bring practical problem-solving, principal-level involvement, and a deep respect for the communities our clients serve.

Our point of view

A better project is a shared achievement.

We are at our best when structure, site, budget, schedule, and construction are considered together. That is how engineering becomes a source of momentum—not friction.
